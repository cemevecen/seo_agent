Privacy Policy

Effective date: {{ effective_date }}

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ company_name }} (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the {{ app_name }} mobile application.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

  • Account or contact details you choose to provide (for example an email address when you contact support).
  • Device and usage information such as device type, operating system version, app version, language, and crash or diagnostic data needed to keep the app reliable.
  • Content you submit inside the app when a feature asks you to enter text or other material.

We do not ask for more personal information than the features require.

2. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve {{ app_name }}.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To diagnose crashes, abuse, or security issues.
  • To comply with law and App Store requirements.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers that help us host infrastructure, deliver email, or analyze crashes — only as needed to run the app, under appropriate confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect users and {{ company_name }}.

4. Retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymize it unless a longer period is required by law.

5. Children’s privacy

{{ app_name }} is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices

You can stop using the app at any time and request deletion of personal data associated with your support requests by emailing us. Device permissions (notifications, photos, etc.) can be changed in iOS Settings.

7. International users

If you use {{ app_name }} from outside the country where we operate, your information may be processed in that country or where our providers run services.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
